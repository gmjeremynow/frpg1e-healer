# Healer (The Dragon #3) for 1e FRPG, v0.2.0

An optional class module for the 1e FRPG system for Foundry VTT (system 0.36.0 or later). It adds the healer: a lawful or neutral class, part magic-user and part cleric with a little fighter, built to patch up other characters quickly. It has its own seven-level spell list and gains new powers every four levels. In The Dragon #3 the editor says many of these sub-classes are meant as non-player characters. Turn the module on or off in Manage Modules.

Based on C. Hettlestad's article "Healers" (The Dragon #3, October 1976, p.22-23; reprinted in The Best of Dragon vol. II, p.12-13). The rules are restated in plain words; the numbers (the level table, spells per day and spells) follow the article. You need the article for its original text.

## Install

In Foundry's Setup screen, go to Add-on Modules, Install Module, and paste this manifest URL:

`https://github.com/gmjeremynow/frpg1e-healer/releases/latest/download/module.json`

## What it adds

- The class "Healer (The Dragon #3)": the article's titles (Apprentice to Doctor, 25th), d8 hit dice, spells per day at seven spell levels, and four 1st level magic-user spells from 16th level and four 2nd level ones from 20th.
- Its own spell list, "Healer", and a compendium of the 42 spells. Spells the article calls the "same as" a magic-user or cleric spell carry that spell's text; the healer's own spells are restated from the article.
- A Healer tab: title, hit dice, spells per day, race limit, the powers by level (molds and slimes at 4th, potions at 8th, scrolls at 12th, magic-user spells at 16th and 20th) and reminders.
- Record tab notes, which never block anything: intelligence, wisdom or dexterity below 15, a race or level not allowed, a chaotic alignment, more than one class, armor worn.
- A compendium with the article restated in plain words, the level table, and how The Dragon #3 differs from the reprint.

## Choices made here

- Experience points: the cleric's table by default. The world setting "Healer experience table" switches to the article's own (5,000 at 2nd level to 1,900,000 at 25th).
- Hit dice: the table shows "10+1" to "10+15" after 10th level, read here as 10 dice, then +1 hit point a level.
- Not in the article: the cleric's attack table, saving throws, proficiency slots and −3 when not proficient; no bonus spells for wisdom; no turning undead.
- Two printings: the rules and spell list follow the reprint in The Best of Dragon vol. II. The level table and experience are from The Dragon #3, since the reprint leaves them out. The Dragon #3 has raise dead at 3rd level (the reprint: speak with dead) and "raise dead fully" at 5th (the reprint: raise dead), prints "8 to 24" for improved cure serious wounds (the reprint: 8-28) and gives detect disease a range of "30" with no unit (the reprint: 30"); these and a few name changes are listed on the compendium's Spells page.
